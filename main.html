    We are a non-profit organization that aids and assists Filipino communities in poverty. Our goal is to provide Filipinos with different opportunities that can help alleviate their living conditions.

    Our Mission

    To empower communities and develop wholistic minded leaders through programs that will help the poor overcome poverty and develop people’s values, skills and leadership potentials.

    Our Vision

    Communities of people living purposeful and productive lives with integrity, and genuinely caring for others and their environment.
